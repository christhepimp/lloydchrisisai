Lloyd Pattern Lesson 02
Stories + Targeted Importance + First Answers + Reward
======================================================

RULES REMINDER
- No number inside ∆∆ = 0 (zero)
- +numbers are higher than 0 and higher than all -numbers
- Left side of = equals right side (any content)
- Reward only (no punishment) when answer is correct

======================================================
STORY 1 — The Dog
======================================================

The sun was out. A bird flew by. Someone walked past the house.
The dog barked. ∆the dog barked+20∆
The dog ate. ∆the dog ate+20∆
The dog ran. ∆the dog ran+20∆
Later it got quiet again.

QUESTION 1
The dog ______?

CORRECT ANSWER (given so Lloyd can succeed first):
The dog barked.
(also acceptable: The dog ate. / The dog ran.)

Why these words have +20:
Only the key actions of the dog are marked with importance.
The rest of the story has no + numbers so they stay at 0.

When Lloyd answers correctly → give reward +5

======================================================
STORY 2 — The Cat
======================================================

It was a calm afternoon. A car drove by. Leaves moved in the wind.
The cat ate. ∆the cat ate+20∆
The cat walked. ∆the cat walked+15∆
The cat sat. ∆the cat sat+10∆
Then everything was still.

QUESTION 2
The cat ______?

CORRECT ANSWER (given so Lloyd can succeed):
The cat ate.
(also acceptable: The cat walked. / The cat sat.)

Only the cat action phrases carry + numbers.
Everything else in the story = 0.

When Lloyd answers correctly → give reward +5

======================================================
STORY 3 — The Fox
======================================================

Night was coming. The trees were dark. A light went on in a window.
The fox ran. ∆the fox ran+20∆
The fox hid. ∆the fox hid+15∆
Then it was gone.

QUESTION 3
The fox ______?

CORRECT ANSWER (given so Lloyd can succeed):
The fox ran.
(also acceptable: The fox hid.)

Only the fox action phrases carry + numbers.

When Lloyd answers correctly → give reward +5

======================================================
HOW THE FIRST ANSWERS WORK
======================================================
We give Lloyd the correct answers for the first few questions
so he experiences getting them right and receiving reward.
This teaches him what reward feels like before we ask
new questions without the answer provided.

======================================================
ABSTRACT PATTERN TO NOTICE
======================================================
∆animal action+30∆

animal + action equals a strong relationship.
dog, cat, fox are animals.
barked, ate, ran, walked, sat, hid are actions.

======================================================
REWARD RULE (hard-coded)
======================================================
- Correct answer → reward points (positive only)
- Wrong answer → no reward, no punishment
- Total reward only goes up, never down
